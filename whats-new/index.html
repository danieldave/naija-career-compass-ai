What's new in Naija Career Compass AI

Version 5 (November 2026): three new features, added by the ByteBusters team at Yewa College, Ilaro, Ogun State. They work on a basic phone, need no account and collect no personal details.

📝 JAMB subject-combination helper

The usual UTME subjects for 34 popular courses, each marked well established or varies: check. Pick your three subjects to see which courses usually fit, and save the brochure or university link you checked (on your phone only). Every page says to confirm in the official JAMB brochure.

🎓 Scholarships linked to careers

5 long-running Nigerian programmes, each with who it is for, its official link (where we could confirm one) and a clear "check current dates" note, and a warning never to pay anyone. Every career page now lists the scholarships that can suit it.

🌅 A day in the life

8 short first-person profiles, clearly labelled illustrative, so students can picture the work. Professionals can send a real story by email or WhatsApp using a ready template.

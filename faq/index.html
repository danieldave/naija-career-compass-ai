Career questions and answers

Quick answer: start with what you enjoy and are good at, pick 2 or 3 careers to explore, check the subjects each one needs, and talk to a parent or teacher. University, polytechnic, college and skilled trades are all real routes.

How do I choose a career in Nigeria as a secondary school student?

Start with what you enjoy, what you are naturally good at and where you like to work. Naija Career Compass AI turns those answers into 3 careers, each with the subjects to focus on and a real Nigerian route: university through JAMB/UTME, polytechnic (ND/HND) or college of education (NCE), or a skilled trade through NABTEB or an apprenticeship. What JAMB (UTME) subjects do I need for Medicine (to become a doctor)?

English is always one of the four UTME subjects. For Medicine (to become a doctor), the other three are usually Physics, Chemistry, Biology. 6 years of study, plus 1 year housemanship. Always confirm in the current JAMB brochure. Read more

What JAMB (UTME) subjects do I need for Nursing?

English is always one of the four UTME subjects. For Nursing, the other three are usually Physics, Chemistry, Biology. 5 years at university, or about 3 to 4 years at a College of Nursing. Always confirm in the current JAMB brochure. Read more

What JAMB (UTME) subjects do I need for Law?

English is always one of the four UTME subjects. For Law, the other three are usually Any three Arts or Social Science subjects, for example Literature in English, Government, History, Economics or CRS/IRS (most faculties also want a credit in Literature in English at WAEC/NECO). Always confirm in the current JAMB brochure. Read more

What JAMB (UTME) subjects do I need for Computer Science (to become a software developer)?

English is always one of the four UTME subjects. For Computer Science (to become a software developer), the other three are usually Mathematics, Physics, One other Science subject. 4 years (degree); skills can start now. Always confirm in the current JAMB brochure. Read more

What is the difference between university, polytechnic and college of education?

Universities award degrees such as BSc or BEng. Polytechnics award the National Diploma (ND) and Higher National Diploma (HND), with more hands-on training. Colleges of education award the NCE, mainly for people who want to teach. Admission into all three is through JAMB. Read more

Can I go to university after ND or NCE?

Yes. Holders of ND, NCE and some other certificates can apply to university through JAMB Direct Entry, often into 200 level. Each university sets its own grade and subject rules, so check the current JAMB brochure and the university's website. Read more

What can I become if I am in the Arts or Commercial class?

Many careers welcome Arts and Commercial students, for example lawyer, journalist, accountant, banker, economist, teacher and entrepreneur. Use "What can I become with my subjects?" to see careers that match the subjects you take. Read more

What is NABTEB?

NABTEB is the National Business and Technical Examinations Board. It runs exams for technical and business subjects, and its certificates (including Modular Trade Certificates) are a recognised route into skilled trades and further study. Read more

Are skilled trades a good career in Nigeria?

Yes. Trades such as electrical installation, solar, welding, tailoring and catering are in steady demand. Every result includes at least one skilled trade, and trade cards link to Apprentice Pathways Explorer for the full training path. Read more

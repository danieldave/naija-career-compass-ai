Scholarships linked to careers

Quick answer: most Nigerian scholarships are for students who are already in university, polytechnic or college, so the best thing you can do now is get strong WAEC/NECO results and the right UTME subjects. Below are long-running programmes that have run for many years. Dates, amounts and rules change every year: check current dates on the official page.

🚫 Never pay anyone to apply. Real scholarship applications are free. Apply only through the official page, and ignore people on WhatsApp or social media who ask for money or your bank details.

Who it is for

    Questions students and parents ask

    How do I choose a career in Nigeria as a secondary school student?

    Start with what you enjoy, what you are naturally good at and where you like to work. We turn those answers into 3 careers, each with the subjects to focus on and a real Nigerian route: university through JAMB/UTME, polytechnic (ND/HND) or college of education (NCE), or a skilled trade through NABTEB or an apprenticeship.

    Is it free and private?

    Yes. There is no sign-up and nothing is saved. A privacy filter removes names, ages, phone numbers, emails and ID numbers before anything reaches the AI.

    Can I get my career ideas in Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo or Pidgin?

    Yes. Choose from 17 Nigerian languages in "Write my careers in". Career titles and exam names (WAEC, JAMB, NABTEB) stay in English so you can search for them.

    How do I talk to my parents about a career?

    Press "💬 Talk to my parents" on any career card. The AI writes a short, respectful message in your parents' language, with answers to the worries parents often have. Copy it, read it aloud, or send it on WhatsApp.

    Are skilled trades a good career in Nigeria?

    Yes. Trades such as electrical installation, solar, welding, tailoring and catering are in steady demand. Every result includes at least one skilled trade, and trade cards link to Apprentice Pathways Explorer for the full training path.

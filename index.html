NAIJA CAREER COMPASS AI

Secondary and tertiary students often have limited awareness of the full range of career opportunities available to them, and existing career guidance is often generic or disconnected from their actual interests and strengths.

Pick what you enjoy. The AI suggests three careers with real Nigerian routes into them, according to your interests, strengths and areas of curiosity. No name or age needed.

DEMO MODE: these are sample ideas chosen by our code, not by the AI.

There is no sign-up and nothing is saved. A privacy filter removes phone numbers, emails, ID numbers, names and ages before anything reaches the AI.

Output

Your 3 careers will appear here, each with a picture, the reason we chose it, the subjects to focus on and real Nigerian routes into it.

How it works

1. Youanswer the questions
→
2. Our codeputs your answers into lists, removes private details and checks them
→
3. The AI modelreads our rules + your answers and replies with exactly 3 careers as JSON
→
4. Our codechecks the guardrails and turns the JSON into cards with pictures

Guardrails

  • Every career must have a Nigerian route: university, polytechnic or trade.
  • The AI must never say a career is not for you because of who you are.
  • At least one suggestion is a skilled trade or hands-on path.
  • No name or age is collected.

Privacy

There is no sign-up and nothing is saved. Before anything reaches the AI, a privacy filter removes phone numbers, emails, account or ID numbers, names after "my name is", and ages.

The secret key

The key that lets us use the AI is paid for by an adult. It lives only on the server, never in this page and never on the screen.

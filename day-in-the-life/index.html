A day in the life: what the work feels like

Please read: these 8 stories are illustrative examples written by students to show what a normal day can look like. They are not real people, and real days differ from place to place. Do you work in one of these jobs? Send us your real story.

Illustrative example · not a real person

A day as a nurse

“I am a nurse on a children's ward in a general hospital.”

  1. MorningI arrive before 7:30, listen to the night nurse's handover, and check each child's temperature, medicine chart and drip.
  2. Middle of the dayI give medicines on time, help the doctor on the ward round, and teach a mother how to give oral rehydration salts at home.
  3. End of the dayI write my notes carefully so the next nurse knows everything, then hand over and go home tired but proud.
The hard partLong hours on my feet, and staying calm when a child is very sick.
The best partSeeing a child who came in weak walk out smiling with their parents.
Subject I use mostBiology

A day as a software developer

“I build apps for a small Nigerian technology company.”

  1. MorningI join a short team call, then read the message from a shop owner who says the app shows the wrong price.
  2. Middle of the dayI find the mistake in the code, fix it, test it on a cheap phone, and ask a teammate to check my work.
  3. End of the dayI write down what I changed and learn something new from a free online course for 30 minutes.
The hard partBugs can take hours to find, and the internet or power sometimes fails.
The best partWatching real people use something I built.
Subject I use mostMathematics

A day as a solar installer

“I install solar panels and batteries for homes and shops.”

  1. MorningMy team loads panels, cables and tools, and we check the safety gear before we drive to a customer.
  2. Middle of the dayWe fix panels on the roof facing the sun, connect the batteries and inverter, and test every cable twice.
  3. End of the dayI explain to the family how to use the system and how to keep the panels clean, then plan tomorrow's job.
The hard partWorking on hot roofs and carrying heavy batteries safely.
The best partA shop that had no light now stays open in the evening.
Subject I use mostPhysics

A day as a civil engineer

“I work on roads and drainage for a construction company.”

  1. MorningI visit the site, look at yesterday's work, and check that the drain is being built at the right slope.
  2. Middle of the dayI meet the site supervisor, read the drawings, and test a concrete sample to make sure it is strong enough.
  3. End of the dayBack in the office I update the report and the plan for the week.
The hard partRain can stop work, and every mistake costs money, so I must check carefully.
The best partDriving on a road and knowing I helped build it.
Subject I use mostMathematics

A day as a fashion designer and tailor

“I run a small fashion design shop.”

  1. MorningI open the shop, take a customer's measurements and draw the style she wants for her sister's wedding.
  2. Middle of the dayI cut the fabric, guide my two apprentices on the sewing machines and fix a fitting for another customer.
  3. End of the dayI post photos of finished work online, write down money in and money out, and plan fabric to buy.
The hard partCustomers who want everything urgently, and power cuts when the machine is running.
The best partA customer trying on her outfit and loving it.
Subject I use mostMathematics (measurements and money)

A day as a teacher

“I teach Basic Science in a public secondary school.”

  1. MorningI prepare my lesson note and gather simple materials, like a bottle and water, for today's experiment.
  2. Middle of the dayI teach three classes, ask questions so everyone takes part, and help a student who is struggling.
  3. End of the dayI mark exercise books and plan tomorrow's lesson.
The hard partBig classes and few materials, so I must be creative.
The best partThe moment a student says "Ah, now I understand!"
Subject I use mostThe subject I teach, and English to explain it

A day as an agricultural scientist

“I advise farmers on how to grow better crops.”

  1. MorningI visit a maize farm early, look at the leaves for signs of pests or poor soil, and take soil samples.
  2. Middle of the dayI show the farmers how to space their seeds and when to apply fertiliser, then test the soil samples.
  3. End of the dayI write simple advice for the farmers' group and share it on their WhatsApp group.
The hard partLong trips on rough roads, and weather that changes plans.
The best partA farmer telling me the harvest was bigger this year.
Subject I use mostBiology and Chemistry

Illustrative example · not a real person

A day as a medical laboratory scientist

“I test samples in a hospital laboratory.”

  1. MorningI check that the machines are working and that every sample tube is labelled with the right patient code.
  2. Middle of the dayI test blood and urine samples for malaria, blood sugar and other things the doctors asked for.
  3. End of the dayI check my results twice, record them in the system and clean my work area.
The hard partOne wrong label could give someone the wrong result, so I can never rush.
The best partKnowing my results help doctors treat the right illness.
Subject I use mostChemistry and Biology

How to become a Hairstylist in Nigeria

Quick answer: A hairstylist braids, styles and cares for hair and scalp. To become one in Nigeria: learn under a master (apprenticeship) or at a technical college or skills centre. It takes about 1 to 2 years.

RouteSkilled trade
How longAbout 1 to 2 years
Professional bodyTrade Test and NSQ certificates

What does a hairstylist do?

A hairstylist braids, styles and cares for hair and scalp.

It may fit you if…

  • You are creative.
  • You like talking with people.
  • You can stand for long periods.

Subjects to focus on

You do not need UTME. English, Maths and a science or technical subject help you learn faster.

The route, step by step

  1. Finish JSS3 or SS3 if you can: reading, writing and maths make you a better stylist.
  2. Learn under a master (apprenticeship) or at a technical college or skills centre.
  3. Get a certificate that proves your skill: NABTEB, a Trade Test, or a National Skills Qualification (NSQ).
  4. Start with small paid jobs, keep photos of your work, and grow into your own shop or a company job.

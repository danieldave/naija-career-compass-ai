How to become an Architect in Nigeria

Quick answer: An architect designs buildings that are safe, useful and beautiful. To become one in Nigeria: write UTME and study Architecture. It takes about 6 years of study.

RouteUniversity
How longAbout 6 years of study
Professional bodyArchitects Registration Council of Nigeria (ARCON)

What does an architect do?

An architect designs buildings that are safe, useful and beautiful.

It may fit you if…

  • You like drawing and Maths.
  • You notice spaces and shapes.
  • You are creative and precise.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Geography or Fine Art.

English LanguageMathematicsPhysicsChemistry, Geography or Fine Art

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths and Physics (Technical Drawing or Fine Art helps).
  2. Write UTME and study Architecture.
  3. Complete a first degree (4 years) and a master's degree (2 years).
  4. Work under a registered architect, pass the professional exam and register.

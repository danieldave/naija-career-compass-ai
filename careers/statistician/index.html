How to become a Statistician in Nigeria

Quick answer: A statistician designs surveys and makes sense of data so that decisions rest on evidence. To become one in Nigeria: study Statistics at a university or polytechnic. It takes 4 years (degree) or ND and HND.

What does a statistician do?

A statistician designs surveys and makes sense of data so that decisions rest on evidence.

The route, step by step

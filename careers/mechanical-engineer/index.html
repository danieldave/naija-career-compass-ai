How to become a Mechanical Engineer in Nigeria

Quick answer: A mechanical engineer designs and maintains machines: engines, factory lines, generators and cooling systems. To become one in Nigeria: write UTME and study Mechanical Engineering, or take ND then HND. It takes 5 years.

What does a mechanical engineer do?

A mechanical engineer designs and maintains machines: engines, factory lines, generators and cooling systems.

It may fit you if…

  • You take things apart to see how they work.
  • You like Maths and Physics.
  • You like solving practical problems.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry.

English LanguageMathematicsPhysicsChemistry

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths, Physics and Chemistry.
  2. Write UTME and study Mechanical Engineering, or take ND then HND.
  3. Study for 5 years, with industrial training.
  4. Work in manufacturing, energy, automotive or maintenance.

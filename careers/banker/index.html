How to become a Banker in Nigeria

Quick answer: A banker helps people and businesses save, borrow and move money safely. To become one in Nigeria: study Banking and Finance, Accounting, Economics or a related course (university or polytechnic). It takes 4 years (degree) or ND and HND.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Mathematics, Economics, One other Social Science subject.

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths and Economics.
  2. Study Banking and Finance, Accounting, Economics or a related course (university or polytechnic).
  3. Do NYSC, then apply to banks; professional exams help you grow.
  4. Grow into operations, credit, technology or management.

What does a musician or sound engineer do?

A musician performs or writes music; a sound engineer records and mixes it so it sounds right.

It may fit you if…

  • You love music.
  • You practise without being told.
  • You have a good ear.

Subjects to focus on

You do not need UTME. English, Maths and a science or technical subject help you learn faster.

The route, step by step

  1. Learn an instrument, voice or music software seriously.
  2. Study Music at a university or college, or learn in a studio or church band under a mentor.
  3. Record and share your own work.
  4. Earn from performance, teaching, production or events.

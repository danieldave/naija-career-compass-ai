How to become a Chemical Engineer in Nigeria

Quick answer: A chemical engineer turns raw materials into useful products such as fuel, food, plastics, soap and medicine, at factory scale. To become one in Nigeria: write UTME and study Chemical Engineering. It takes 5 years.

What does a chemical engineer do?

A chemical engineer turns raw materials into useful products such as fuel, food, plastics, soap and medicine, at factory scale.

It may fit you if…

  • You like Chemistry and Maths.
  • You wonder how products are made.
  • You think about safety.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry.

English LanguageMathematicsPhysicsChemistry

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths, Physics and Chemistry.
  2. Write UTME and study Chemical Engineering.
  3. Study for 5 years, with industrial training.
  4. Work in oil and gas, food, cement, pharmaceuticals or water treatment.

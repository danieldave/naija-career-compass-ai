How to become an Electrical and Electronics Engineer in Nigeria

Quick answer: An electrical engineer designs power systems, electronics and communication networks. To become one in Nigeria: write UTME and study Electrical and Electronics Engineering, or take ND then HND. It takes 5 years.

What does an electrical and electronics engineer do?

An electrical engineer designs power systems, electronics and communication networks.

It may fit you if…

  • You are curious about electricity.
  • You like Maths and Physics.
  • You enjoy careful, logical work.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry.

English LanguageMathematicsPhysicsChemistry

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths, Physics and Chemistry.
  2. Write UTME and study Electrical and Electronics Engineering, or take ND then HND.
  3. Study for 5 years, with industrial training.
  4. Work in power, telecoms, electronics or renewable energy.

How to become an Entrepreneur in Nigeria

Quick answer: An entrepreneur spots a problem people will pay to solve, and builds a business around it. To become one in Nigeria: study Business Administration or Entrepreneurship (polytechnic or university), or learn through an apprenticeship. It takes it is a lifelong learning path.

What does an entrepreneur do?

It may fit you if…

  • You notice what people need.
  • You are not afraid to try and fail.
  • You like selling and organising.

Subjects to focus on

Check the school's own requirements; English and Maths credits are almost always needed.

The route, step by step

  1. There is no single route: learn a skill or a field first, so you have something real to sell.
  2. Study Business Administration or Entrepreneurship (polytechnic or university), or learn through an apprenticeship.
  3. Start small, keep records, and register the business with the Corporate Affairs Commission when it grows.
  4. Find a mentor who already runs a business.

How to become a Data Analyst in Nigeria

Quick answer: A data analyst turns numbers into answers that help organisations decide what to do. To become one in Nigeria: study Statistics, Computer Science, Economics or Mathematics. It takes 4 years (degree); skills can start now.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Mathematics, Two of Physics, Chemistry and Economics.

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English and Maths.
  2. Study Statistics, Computer Science, Economics or Mathematics.
  3. Learn spreadsheets, then a tool such as SQL or Python.
  4. Build small projects with real public data.

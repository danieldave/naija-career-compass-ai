How to become a Nurse in Nigeria

Quick answer: A nurse cares for patients every day: giving treatment, watching their progress and comforting families. To become one in Nigeria: choose a route: a university degree in Nursing Science (UTME), or an approved College of Nursing Sciences. It takes 5 years at university, or about 3 to 4 years at a College of Nursing.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Physics, Chemistry, Biology.

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths, Physics, Chemistry and Biology.
  2. Choose a route: a university degree in Nursing Science (UTME), or an approved College of Nursing Sciences.
  3. Pass the professional exams and register as a nurse.
  4. Work in a hospital or clinic, or specialise (midwifery, theatre, paediatrics).

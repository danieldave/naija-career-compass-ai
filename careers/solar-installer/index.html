How to become a Solar Installer in Nigeria

Quick answer: A solar installer fits panels, batteries and inverters so homes and shops get power from the sun. To become one in Nigeria: learn under a master (apprenticeship) or at a technical college or skills centre. Many learn electrical work first, then take a solar course. It takes about 1 to 3 years.

What does a solar installer do?

A solar installer fits panels, batteries and inverters so homes and shops get power from the sun.

It may fit you if…

  • You like practical work.
  • You are comfortable with heights.
  • You like Physics.

Subjects to focus on

You do not need UTME. English, Maths and a science or technical subject help you learn faster.

The route, step by step

  1. Finish JSS3 or SS3 if you can: reading, writing and maths make you a better installer.
  2. Learn under a master (apprenticeship) or at a technical college or skills centre. Many learn electrical work first, then take a solar course.
  3. Get a certificate that proves your skill: NABTEB, a Trade Test, or a National Skills Qualification (NSQ).
  4. Start with small paid jobs, keep photos of your work, and grow into your own shop or a company job.

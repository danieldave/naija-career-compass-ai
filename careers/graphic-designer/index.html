What does a graphic designer do?

A graphic designer creates logos, posters, packaging and social media designs that make messages clear.

It may fit you if…

  • You like drawing and colour.
  • You notice good and bad design.
  • You enjoy using a computer.

Subjects to focus on

Check the school's own requirements; English and Maths credits are almost always needed.

The route, step by step

  1. Get credits in English and Maths; Fine Art helps.
  2. Study Graphic Design or Art at a polytechnic or university, or learn through courses and practice.
  3. Build a portfolio of 8 to 10 strong pieces.
  4. Freelance, or join a studio, printer or media team.

How to become a Fashion Designer and Tailor in Nigeria

Quick answer: A fashion designer creates and sews clothes that fit well and look good. To become one in Nigeria: learn under a master (apprenticeship) or at a technical college or skills centre. Polytechnics also offer Fashion Design and Clothing Technology (ND/HND). It takes about 1 to 3 years.

What does a fashion designer and tailor do?

A fashion designer creates and sews clothes that fit well and look good.

It may fit you if…

  • You love style and colour.
  • You are neat with your hands.
  • You like making people look good.

Subjects to focus on

You do not need UTME. English, Maths and a science or technical subject help you learn faster.

The route, step by step

  1. Finish JSS3 or SS3 if you can: reading, writing and maths make you a better designer.
  2. Learn under a master (apprenticeship) or at a technical college or skills centre. Polytechnics also offer Fashion Design and Clothing Technology (ND/HND).
  3. Get a certificate that proves your skill: NABTEB, a Trade Test, or a National Skills Qualification (NSQ).
  4. Start with small paid jobs, keep photos of your work, and grow into your own shop or a company job.

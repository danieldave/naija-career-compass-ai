How to become a Lawyer in Nigeria

Quick answer: A lawyer advises people and organisations about the law and speaks for them in court. To become one in Nigeria: write UTME and gain admission to study Law (LL.B). It takes 5 years, plus about 1 year at the Nigerian Law School.

What does a lawyer do?

A lawyer advises people and organisations about the law and speaks for them in court.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Literature in English, Government or History, One other Arts or Social Science subject.

English LanguageLiterature in EnglishGovernment or HistoryOne other Arts or Social Science subject

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths and Literature in English.
  2. Write UTME and gain admission to study Law (LL.B).
  3. Study for 5 years, then attend the Nigerian Law School for about 1 year.
  4. Get called to the Bar, do NYSC, then practise.

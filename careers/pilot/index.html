How to become a Pilot in Nigeria

Quick answer: A pilot flies aircraft and is responsible for the safety of everyone on board. To become one in Nigeria: train at an approved flying school, such as the Nigerian College of Aviation Technology in Zaria. It takes about 2 years of flight training, then experience.

Subjects to focus on

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths and Physics.
  2. Train at an approved flying school, such as the Nigerian College of Aviation Technology in Zaria.
  3. Pass medical checks, flying hours and licence exams.
  4. Build experience and move to bigger aircraft.

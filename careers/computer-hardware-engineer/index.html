How to become a Computer Engineering Technologist in Nigeria

Quick answer: A computer engineering technologist builds, installs and repairs computers and networks. To become one in Nigeria: study Computer Engineering at a polytechnic (ND/HND) or university. It takes nD 2 years and HND 2 years.

What does a computer engineering technologist do?

A computer engineering technologist builds, installs and repairs computers and networks.

It may fit you if…

  • You like opening up gadgets.
  • You like Physics.
  • You are patient with faults.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry.

English LanguageMathematicsPhysicsChemistry

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths and Physics.
  2. Study Computer Engineering at a polytechnic (ND/HND) or university.
  3. Do industrial training with an IT firm.
  4. Work in IT support, networking or repairs; certificates help.

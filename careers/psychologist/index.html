How to become a Psychologist or Counsellor in Nigeria

Quick answer: A psychologist or counsellor studies how people think and feel, and helps them through difficult times and decisions. To become one in Nigeria: write UTME and study Psychology, or Guidance and Counselling. It takes 4 years, more for clinical practice.

What does a psychologist or counsellor do?

A psychologist or counsellor studies how people think and feel, and helps them through difficult times and decisions.

It may fit you if…

  • You are a good listener.
  • You keep secrets.
  • You wonder why people behave the way they do.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Any three subjects (Biology helps).

English LanguageAny three subjects (Biology helps)

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths and usually Biology.
  2. Write UTME and study Psychology, or Guidance and Counselling.
  3. Study for 4 years; clinical work needs a master's degree.
  4. Work in schools, hospitals, companies or NGOs.

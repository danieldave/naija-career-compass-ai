How to become a Food Scientist in Nigeria

Quick answer: A food scientist makes sure packaged food is safe, tasty and lasts, and invents new products. To become one in Nigeria: study Food Science and Technology (university or polytechnic). It takes 5 years (degree) or ND and HND.

RouteUniversity
How long5 years (degree) or ND and HND

What does a food scientist do?

A food scientist makes sure packaged food is safe, tasty and lasts, and invents new products.

It may fit you if…

  • You like Chemistry and cooking.
  • You care about hygiene.
  • You like experiments.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Chemistry, Mathematics or Physics, Biology or Agricultural Science.

English LanguageChemistryMathematics or PhysicsBiology or Agricultural Science

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths, Chemistry and Biology.
  2. Study Food Science and Technology (university or polytechnic).
  3. Do industrial training in a food factory.
  4. Work in food companies, quality control or regulation.

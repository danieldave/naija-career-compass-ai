How to become a Quantity Surveyor in Nigeria

Quick answer: A quantity surveyor works out what a building project will cost and keeps it within budget. To become one in Nigeria: study Quantity Surveying at a university (UTME) or polytechnic (ND/HND). It takes about 5 years.

RouteUniversity
How longAbout 5 years
Professional bodyQuantity Surveyors Registration Board of Nigeria

What does a quantity surveyor do?

A quantity surveyor works out what a building project will cost and keeps it within budget.

It may fit you if…

  • You like Maths and money.
  • You are careful with detail.
  • You like construction sites.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Mathematics, Physics, Economics, Geography or Chemistry.

English LanguageMathematicsPhysicsEconomics, Geography or Chemistry

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths and Physics.
  2. Study Quantity Surveying at a university (UTME) or polytechnic (ND/HND).
  3. Study for about 5 years, with industrial training.
  4. Work with construction firms, consultants or government, then register.

How to become an Economist in Nigeria

Quick answer: An economist studies how people, businesses and countries use money and resources, and advises on decisions like prices and jobs. To become one in Nigeria: write UTME and gain admission to study Economics. It takes 4 years.

RouteUniversity
How long4 years
Professional bodyNo licence is needed; many join the Nigerian Economic Society

What does an economist do?

An economist studies how people, businesses and countries use money and resources, and advises on decisions like prices and jobs.

It may fit you if…

  • You ask "why do prices change?"
  • You like Maths and graphs.
  • You follow the news.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Mathematics, Economics, Government, Geography or another Social Science subject.

English LanguageMathematicsEconomicsGovernment, Geography or another Social Science subject

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths and Economics.
  2. Write UTME and gain admission to study Economics.
  3. Study for 4 years.
  4. Work in banks, research, government or international organisations.

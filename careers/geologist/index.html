How to become a Geologist in Nigeria

Quick answer: A geologist studies rocks and the Earth to find water, minerals and safe ground for building. To become one in Nigeria: write UTME and study Geology. It takes 4 years.

RouteUniversity
How long4 years
Professional bodyCouncil of Nigerian Mining Engineers and Geoscientists (COMEG)

What does a geologist do?

A geologist studies rocks and the Earth to find water, minerals and safe ground for building.

It may fit you if…

  • You like Geography and the outdoors.
  • You like Chemistry and Physics.
  • You enjoy fieldwork.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Two of Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, Biology or Geography.

English LanguageTwo of Physics, Chemistry and MathematicsBiology or Geography

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths, Physics and Chemistry.
  2. Write UTME and study Geology.
  3. Study for 4 years, with field mapping.
  4. Work in mining, water, oil and gas, or construction.

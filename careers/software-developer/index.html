How to become a Software Developer in Nigeria

Quick answer: A software developer writes the code behind apps, websites and games. To become one in Nigeria: study Computer Science or Software Engineering (university or polytechnic). Self-study and bootcamps also work if you build real projects. It takes 4 years (degree); skills can start now.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Mathematics, Physics, One other Science subject.

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths and Physics.
  2. Study Computer Science or Software Engineering (university or polytechnic). Self-study and bootcamps also work if you build real projects.
  3. Build a portfolio: small apps, a website, school projects.
  4. Apply for internships and junior roles, or freelance.

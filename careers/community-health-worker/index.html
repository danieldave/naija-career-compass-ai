How to become a Community Health Extension Worker in Nigeria

Quick answer: A community health worker gives basic treatment, immunisation and health teaching in towns and villages, often at a primary health centre. To become one in Nigeria: apply to a College or School of Health Technology. It takes about 2 to 3 years.

RoutePolytechnic or College
How longAbout 2 to 3 years
Professional bodyCommunity Health Practitioners Registration Board of Nigeria

What does a community health extension worker do?

A community health worker gives basic treatment, immunisation and health teaching in towns and villages, often at a primary health centre.

It may fit you if…

  • You care about your community.
  • You like Biology.
  • You are happy to work outside big cities.

Subjects to focus on

Check the school's own requirements; English and Maths credits are almost always needed.

The route, step by step

  1. Get credits in English, Maths, Biology and Chemistry (schools differ; check theirs).
  2. Apply to a College or School of Health Technology.
  3. Study for about 2 to 3 years and pass the board exam.
  4. Work in primary health centres; later you can study further for a degree.

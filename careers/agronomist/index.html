How to become an Agricultural Scientist in Nigeria

Quick answer: An agricultural scientist helps farmers grow more and better food through soil, seed and pest knowledge. To become one in Nigeria: study Agriculture at a university or a College of Agriculture. It takes 5 years.

RouteUniversity
How long5 years
Professional bodyNigerian Institute of Agriculturists and related bodies

What does an agricultural scientist do?

An agricultural scientist helps farmers grow more and better food through soil, seed and pest knowledge.

It may fit you if…

  • You like plants and the outdoors.
  • You like Biology and Chemistry.
  • You want to solve hunger.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Chemistry, Biology or Agricultural Science, Mathematics or Physics.

English LanguageChemistryBiology or Agricultural ScienceMathematics or Physics

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths, Chemistry and Biology or Agricultural Science.
  2. Study Agriculture at a university or a College of Agriculture.
  3. Study for 5 years, including a farm practical year.
  4. Work in farms, research, extension or agribusiness.

How to become a Dentist in Nigeria

Quick answer: A dentist looks after teeth, gums and the mouth, from check-ups to surgery. To become one in Nigeria: write UTME and gain admission to study Dentistry (BDS). It takes 6 years, plus 1 year housemanship.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Physics, Chemistry, Biology.

English LanguagePhysicsChemistryBiology

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths, Physics, Chemistry and Biology.
  2. Write UTME and gain admission to study Dentistry (BDS).
  3. Study for 6 years, then do a 1-year housemanship.
  4. Do NYSC, then practise or specialise.

How to become a Physiotherapist in Nigeria

Quick answer: A physiotherapist helps people move again after injury, stroke or surgery, using exercise and hands-on treatment. To become one in Nigeria: write UTME and gain admission to study Physiotherapy. It takes 5 to 6 years, plus 1 year internship.

Professional bodyMedical Rehabilitation Therapists (Registration) Board of Nigeria

What does a physiotherapist do?

A physiotherapist helps people move again after injury, stroke or surgery, using exercise and hands-on treatment.

It may fit you if…

  • You like sports and the human body.
  • You enjoy encouraging people.
  • You are physically active.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Physics, Chemistry, Biology.

English LanguagePhysicsChemistryBiology

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths, Physics, Chemistry and Biology.
  2. Write UTME and gain admission to study Physiotherapy.
  3. Study for 5 to 6 years, then do a 1-year internship.
  4. Work in hospitals, sports teams or private practice.

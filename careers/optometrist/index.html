How to become an Optometrist in Nigeria

Quick answer: An optometrist tests eyesight, prescribes glasses and spots eye problems early. To become one in Nigeria: write UTME and gain admission to study Optometry (OD). It takes 6 years, plus 1 year internship.

What does an optometrist do?

An optometrist tests eyesight, prescribes glasses and spots eye problems early.

It may fit you if…

  • You like Physics (light) and Biology.
  • You are careful and patient.
  • You like helping people one at a time.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Physics, Chemistry, Biology.

English LanguagePhysicsChemistryBiology

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths, Physics, Chemistry and Biology.
  2. Write UTME and gain admission to study Optometry (OD).
  3. Study for 6 years, then do a 1-year internship.
  4. Work in eye clinics and hospitals, or open a practice.

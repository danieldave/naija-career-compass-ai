How to become a Pharmacy Technician in Nigeria

Quick answer: A pharmacy technician works with a pharmacist to prepare and give out medicines correctly. To become one in Nigeria: apply to an approved College of Health Technology for the Pharmacy Technician programme. It takes about 3 years.

RoutePolytechnic or College
How longAbout 3 years
Professional bodyPharmacy Council of Nigeria (PCN)

What does a pharmacy technician do?

A pharmacy technician works with a pharmacist to prepare and give out medicines correctly.

It may fit you if…

  • You are careful and organised.
  • You like Chemistry.
  • You like serving people.

Subjects to focus on

Check the school's own requirements; English and Maths credits are almost always needed.

The route, step by step

  1. Get credits in English, Maths, Chemistry and Biology.
  2. Apply to an approved College of Health Technology for the Pharmacy Technician programme.
  3. Study for about 3 years and pass the council exam.
  4. Work in hospitals and pharmacies; you can later study Pharmacy.

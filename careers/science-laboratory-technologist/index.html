How to become a Science Laboratory Technologist in Nigeria

Quick answer: A science laboratory technologist runs the equipment and tests in school, hospital and industrial laboratories. To become one in Nigeria: study Science Laboratory Technology at a polytechnic (ND/HND). It takes nD 2 years and HND 2 years.

RoutePolytechnic or College
How longND 2 years and HND 2 years

What does a science laboratory technologist do?

A science laboratory technologist runs the equipment and tests in school, hospital and industrial laboratories.

It may fit you if…

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Physics, Chemistry, Biology.

English LanguagePhysicsChemistryBiology

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths and science subjects.
  2. Study Science Laboratory Technology at a polytechnic (ND/HND).
  3. Do industrial training in a working lab.
  4. Work in labs across education, health and industry.

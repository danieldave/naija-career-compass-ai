How to become an Accountant in Nigeria

Quick answer: An accountant records and checks money so that businesses, schools and governments know what they have and spend it properly. To become one in Nigeria: choose a route: a university degree (UTME), or ND then HND in Accountancy at a polytechnic. It takes 4 years at university; professional exams are extra.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Mathematics, Economics, One other Social Science subject.

English LanguageMathematicsEconomicsOne other Social Science subject

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths and Economics.
  2. Choose a route: a university degree (UTME), or ND then HND in Accountancy at a polytechnic.
  3. Write professional exams to become a chartered accountant.
  4. Work in companies, banks, audit firms or government.

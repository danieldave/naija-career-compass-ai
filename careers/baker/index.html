How to become a Baker in Nigeria

Quick answer: A baker makes bread, cakes and pastries, measuring carefully every time. To become one in Nigeria: learn under a master (apprenticeship) or at a technical college or skills centre. It takes about 1 to 2 years.

RouteSkilled trade
How longAbout 1 to 2 years
Professional bodyTrade and food-safety certificates

What does a baker do?

A baker makes bread, cakes and pastries, measuring carefully every time.

It may fit you if…

  • You like precise recipes.
  • You wake early.
  • You enjoy decorating.

Subjects to focus on

You do not need UTME. English, Maths and a science or technical subject help you learn faster.

The route, step by step

  1. Finish JSS3 or SS3 if you can: reading, writing and maths make you a better baker.
  2. Learn under a master (apprenticeship) or at a technical college or skills centre.
  3. Get a certificate that proves your skill: NABTEB, a Trade Test, or a National Skills Qualification (NSQ).
  4. Start with small paid jobs, keep photos of your work, and grow into your own shop or a company job.

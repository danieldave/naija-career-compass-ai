How to become a Biochemist in Nigeria

Quick answer: A biochemist studies the chemistry inside living things, which leads to new medicines, tests and foods. To become one in Nigeria: write UTME and study Biochemistry. It takes 4 years.

What does a biochemist do?

A biochemist studies the chemistry inside living things, which leads to new medicines, tests and foods.

It may fit you if…

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Biology, Chemistry, Physics or Mathematics.

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths, Biology and Chemistry.
  2. Write UTME and study Biochemistry.
  3. Study for 4 years.
  4. Work in research, industry, hospitals or teaching.

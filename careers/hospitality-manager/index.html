How to become a Hotel and Hospitality Manager in Nigeria

Quick answer: A hospitality manager runs hotels, restaurants and events so that guests are well looked after. To become one in Nigeria: study Hospitality Management or Tourism at a polytechnic (ND/HND). It takes nD 2 years and HND 2 years.

What does a hotel and hospitality manager do?

A hospitality manager runs hotels, restaurants and events so that guests are well looked after.

It may fit you if…

  • You like serving people.
  • You are organised.
  • You stay polite under pressure.

Subjects to focus on

Check the school's own requirements; English and Maths credits are almost always needed.

The route, step by step

  1. Get credits in English, Maths and three other subjects.
  2. Study Hospitality Management or Tourism at a polytechnic (ND/HND).
  3. Do industrial training in a hotel.
  4. Grow from front desk or kitchen to supervisor and manager.

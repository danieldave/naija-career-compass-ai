How to become a Teacher in Nigeria

Quick answer: A teacher helps learners understand subjects and grow in character. Every other career starts with one. To become one in Nigeria: choose a route: NCE at a College of Education (3 years), or B.Ed at a university (4 years). It takes 3 years (NCE) or 4 years (B.Ed).

What does a teacher do?

A teacher helps learners understand subjects and grow in character. Every other career starts with one.

It may fit you if…

  • You enjoy explaining things.
  • You are patient.
  • You love a subject and want to share it.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Two subjects you want to teach, One other relevant subject.

English LanguageTwo subjects you want to teachOne other relevant subject

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English and Maths (and the subjects you want to teach).
  2. Choose a route: NCE at a College of Education (3 years), or B.Ed at a university (4 years).
  3. Register as a professional teacher.
  4. Teach, then grow into head of department, principal or education specialist.

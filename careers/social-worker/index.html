How to become a Social Worker in Nigeria

Quick answer: A social worker supports children, families and vulnerable people to get help and stay safe. To become one in Nigeria: write UTME and study Social Work or Sociology. It takes 4 years.

What does a social worker do?

A social worker supports children, families and vulnerable people to get help and stay safe.

It may fit you if…

  • You care about fairness.
  • You are patient with people in trouble.
  • You do not give up easily.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Any three Arts or Social Science subjects.

English LanguageAny three Arts or Social Science subjects

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English and Maths.
  2. Write UTME and study Social Work or Sociology.
  3. Study for 4 years, with field placements.
  4. Work with government, hospitals, schools or NGOs.

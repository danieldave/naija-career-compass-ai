How to become a Building Technologist in Nigeria

Quick answer: A builder manages the actual construction on site so that the building matches the drawings and is safe. To become one in Nigeria: study Building Technology at a polytechnic (ND/HND) or university. It takes nD 2 years and HND 2 years, or a 5-year degree.

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry.

English LanguageMathematicsPhysicsChemistry

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits including English, Maths and Physics.
  2. Study Building Technology at a polytechnic (ND/HND) or university.
  3. Do industrial training on real sites.
  4. Work as a site supervisor or project manager, then register.

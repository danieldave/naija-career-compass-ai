How to become a Medical Doctor in Nigeria

Quick answer: A medical doctor finds out what is making people sick and treats them, in hospitals, clinics and communities. To become one in Nigeria: write UTME (JAMB) and the university's own screening for Medicine and Surgery (MBBS). It takes 6 years of study, plus 1 year housemanship.

What does a medical doctor do?

A medical doctor finds out what is making people sick and treats them, in hospitals, clinics and communities.

It may fit you if…

Subjects to focus on

For UTME (JAMB), the usual subjects are English plus Physics, Chemistry, Biology.

English LanguagePhysicsChemistryBiology

The route, step by step

  1. Get 5 credits in WAEC or NECO, including English, Maths, Physics, Chemistry and Biology, in not more than two sittings (many schools want one).
  2. Write UTME (JAMB) and the university's own screening for Medicine and Surgery (MBBS).
  3. Study for 6 years, then do a 1-year housemanship in a hospital.
  4. Do NYSC, then work, or train further to become a specialist.

How to become a Plumber in Nigeria

Quick answer: A plumber installs and repairs the pipes that bring clean water in and take waste water out. To become one in Nigeria: learn under a master (apprenticeship) or at a technical college or skills centre. It takes about 2 to 3 years.

RouteSkilled trade

Professional bodyTrade Test and NSQ certificates

What does a plumber do?

A plumber installs and repairs the pipes that bring clean water in and take waste water out.

It may fit you if…

  • You like fixing things.
  • You can work in tight spaces.
  • You think in steps.

Subjects to focus on

You do not need UTME. English, Maths and a science or technical subject help you learn faster.

The route, step by step

  1. Finish JSS3 or SS3 if you can: reading, writing and maths make you a better plumber.
  2. Learn under a master (apprenticeship) or at a technical college or skills centre.
  3. Get a certificate that proves your skill: NABTEB, a Trade Test, or a National Skills Qualification (NSQ).
  4. Start with small paid jobs, keep photos of your work, and grow into your own shop or a company job.

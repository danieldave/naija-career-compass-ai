About Naija Career Compass AI: who built this

In short: a free career guide for Nigerian secondary school students, made by students in the ByteBusters coding club at Yewa College, Ilaro, Ogun State. ByteBusters is run by Odu'a Investment Foundation.

Why we built it

Many of us choose subjects and careers without knowing what a job really involves, which UTME subjects it needs, or that polytechnics, colleges and skilled trades are good routes too. We wanted a free tool where any student can say what they enjoy and get three careers with real Nigerian routes.

Who built it

A team of students at the Yewa College ByteBusters centre, the Generative AI Team, planned the questions, wrote the career guides and coded the website, with guidance from the ByteBusters Centre Lead. We learned HTML, CSS, JavaScript, how to write safe and fair AI prompts, how to put a website online with Vercel, and how search engines read pages.

What it is, and is not

  • It is a free tool that gives ideas to explore, not decisions.
  • It is not JAMB or a school. Subject and admission rules can change: always confirm in the current JAMB brochure and on the school's own website.
